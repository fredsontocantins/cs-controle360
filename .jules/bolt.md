## 2026-09-28 - Operational Record Pre-Fetching for Management Reports
**Learning:** In complex report generation routines (like `ReportGenerator._build_management_report` and `get_consolidated_intelligence`), `list_*` methods were being invoked repeatedly (up to 8-10 times per request) inside helper functions and cycle aggregation windows. Pre-fetching the operational record lists (`homologacao`, `customizacao`, `atividade`, `release`) once at the beginning of the handler/method and passing them into window filtering functions reduced response time by ~22% (7.16ms -> 5.56ms per call).
**Action:** Always audit report and aggregation handlers for repeated `list_*` or repository queries before looping through date windows or sub-metrics. Pre-fetch once and reuse in memory for window calculations.
